## Phase Goal

**As a** user, **I want to** sign in with Google or GitHub on the live stack and stay signed in, **so that** every later feature has a proven foundation.

**This plan's slice:** a release tag puts the exact tested commit on the production domain. Real users can sign in there with Google or GitHub, and the plan does not finish until a real person has done so on both live environments. The VPS facts later phases depend on are proven and recorded: email can leave the box, the environments cannot see each other, and data survives redeploys.

This only matters on staging, because prod normally has no users before the gate. If updated_at does not move on a repeat sign-in and the person's staging accounts pre-date SIGNIN_T0, do not loosen the check. Ask the person before deleting their staging user and its accounts and sessions, then re-run the gate so that both account rows are created during it.
