**As an** invited company admin, **I want to** set up my workspace, team, and product portals, **so that** my customers have a branded place to visit.

**This plan's slice:** the dependency gate. Phase 2's forms, dialogs, toasts, drag reorder, and browser tests all need packages that are not installed yet. </objective>
