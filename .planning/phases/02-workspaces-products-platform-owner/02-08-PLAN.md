## Phase Goal

**As an** invited company admin, **I want to** set up my workspace, team, and product portals, **so that** my customers have a branded place to visit.

**This plan's slice:** products. An admin creates products, each with its own live portal and five starter statuses, then renames them, changes logos, sets a tagline, website, and accent color, and deletes them, while the data and the URL stay reserved.

Then add the create page, overview rows, sidebar product items, and the product settings page (General, Branding with accentTokens + ColorField, Danger zone). Extend the cross-tenant suite to every product route.

Purpose: PROD-01 to PROD-03 and STAT-01; accentTokens and ColorField are reused by the portal (02-10) and the status editor (02-09).
Output: schema, palette constants, products module, product pages and forms, accent library, tests.
</objective>
