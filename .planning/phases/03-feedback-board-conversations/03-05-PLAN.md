## Phase Goal

**As a** customer, **I want to** post, vote on, and discuss ideas on a product's board, **so that** the team sees what I actually need.

**This plan's slice:** voting. The vote box from the tracer becomes a real, optimistic toggle backed by an idempotent PUT/DELETE and an atomic counter (ROADMAP note), so the board ranks by real demand. The shared rate limiter that every later write uses lands here too.

<objective>
Make votes work end to end, prove the counter can't drift under concurrency, add per-user throttling with a proper rate_limited error, and cover the two UI-SPEC vote backstops in the browser.

Purpose: votes are the board's demand signal (core value) and must be race-proof.
Output: votes service and controller, throttle guard and module wiring, 429 mapping, interactive VoteButton, API and browser tests.
</objective>
