## Phase Goal

**As a** customer, **I want to** post, vote on, and discuss ideas on a product's board, **so that** the team sees what I actually need.

**This plan's slice:** posts can change after they are published. Admins change a post's status with a public note that shows up in its timeline, and edit or delete any post from an inline menu on the portal (D-01). Authors edit or delete their own post until someone else engages (D-13, D-14). Every save is protected against a second editor (D-27).

<objective>
Ship the admin status change with its activity log end to end, then author/admin edit and delete with the lock rule and optimistic concurrency, and extend status deletion to reassign posts.

Purpose: MOD-03, POST-05, MOD-01/02 for posts; the activity log is what Phase 4's notifications read.
Output: moderation service and admin controller, edit/delete endpoints, admin and author menus, status dialog, activity rendering, tests.
</objective>
