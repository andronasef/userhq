## Phase Goal

**As a** customer, **I want to** post, vote on, and discuss ideas on a product's board, **so that** the team sees what I actually need.

**This plan's slice:** commenters hear back. When someone else joins a conversation a user is part of, that user gets one daily email per company (D-21) listing what's new, through a DB outbox that can never block or lose a comment (NOTF-03). Development mail lands in Mailpit; production uses the Brevo settings from 03-03.

<objective>
Wire comment → outbox → worker → SMTP → Mailpit end to end, then the batching and recipient rules, retries, and the email's content, escaping, and headers.

Purpose: NOTF-01, NOTF-03, OPS-03. The unsubscribe and mute endpoints that the email links to arrive in 03-13.
Output: env keys, transport, outbox enqueue, worker, renderer, tokens, email module, tests.
</objective>
