## Phase Goal

**As a** customer, **I want to** post, vote on, and discuss ideas on a product's board, **so that** the team sees what I actually need.

**This plan's slice:** the admin's side of the board. Admins get a dashboard table of every post, a detail page with the voter list and the conversation (where they can reply as the team), and a Deleted tab to restore anything removed by mistake (D-01, D-02, D-06, MOD-04).

<objective>
Ship the dashboard board and post detail end to end, then the Deleted tab with restore, then voters on the portal, the dashboard menu variant, filters, and paging.

Purpose: MOD-04, the restore half of MOD-02 (D-06), and D-01/D-02's dashboard surface.
Output: admin list/detail/voters/restore endpoints, dashboard pages and components, tests.
</objective>
