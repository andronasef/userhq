## Phase Goal

**As a** customer, **I want to** post, vote on, and discuss ideas on a product's board, **so that** the team sees what I actually need.

**This plan's slice:** duplicates collapse into one post. Admins merge from the duplicate's page or in bulk from the dashboard; votes combine without double-counting, conversations move intact, and old links keep working (MOD-05, D-03, D-04).

<objective>
Ship the portal merge end to end, then bulk merge from the dashboard with its selection bar, concurrency and dedupe tests, and the merged-URL redirect backstop.

Purpose: MOD-05.
Output: merge service and routes, merge and bulk-merge dialogs, table selection, tests.
</objective>
