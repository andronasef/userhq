| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|

| T-05-13 | Denial of Service / ReDoS | `HighlightText` regex | medium | mitigate | Escape all regex special characters (`replace(/[.*+?^${}()|[\]\\]/g, "\\$&")`) before constructing RegExp. (ASVS V5.2) |
