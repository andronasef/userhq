<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Client to follow routes | A caller-supplied product identity decides whose new-post and new-comment events are delivered to the caller's device |
| Outbox to external push service | A queued row outlives the permissions, preferences and content state that justified it |
| Service worker click handler | A payload url crosses from the push service into navigation of the user's browser |
| Enqueue transaction to Board-disable transaction | Concurrent writers decide whether derived delivery state survives a feature switch |
| Process configuration to production boot | Missing VAPID keys silently degrade into throwaway keys |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-1XS-01 | Information disclosure / Elevation | Open follow route accepting any product UUID | critical | mitigate | Route moved under TenantGuard with product from the slug pair; old route removed; customer, foreign-tenant, unauthenticated and API-key cases tested; routes added to the cross-tenant matrix (Task 3, D-11) |
| T-1XS-02 | Information disclosure | Push sent after a recipient lost access, muted the post, or content became unavailable | high | mitigate | Send-time recheck of preferences, recipient state, Board, post, comment, mute, follow and admin membership with pinned suppression reasons (Task 2, D-02, D-10) |
| T-1XS-03 | Information disclosure | Notification suppressed during a Board-off period delivered after re-enable | high | mitigate | Suppression is terminal, Board disable deletes unsent rows, neither path replays (Tasks 1-2, D-14) |
| T-1XS-04 | Tampering | Enqueue racing the Board-disable cleanup leaves a surviving row | medium | mitigate | Enqueue share-locks the product row until commit; race test in both orderings (Task 1) |
| T-1XS-05 | Spoofing / Tampering | notificationclick opening or navigating to an attacker-chosen origin | medium | mitigate | Resolve against the worker origin, fall back to the origin root on foreign or malformed values, open or focus only, never navigate an existing tab (Task 1) |
| T-1XS-06 | Information disclosure | Removed admin still seeing product name and slug in the followed list | medium | mitigate | Followed list and enqueue join current workspace membership (Task 3, D-11) |
| T-1XS-07 | Denial of service | Production running on throwaway VAPID keys, invalidating all subscriptions on restart | medium | mitigate | Env validation and getVapidKeys both fail in production without keys; compose passes the variables (Task 2) |
| T-1XS-08 | Denial of service | Rows stranded in sending after a crash, and dead subscriptions retried forever | medium | mitigate | Ten-minute visibility-timeout reclaim under SKIP LOCKED; 401 and 403 delete the subscription (Task 2) |
| T-1XS-09 | Repudiation | Suppressed deliveries indistinguishable from failures | low | mitigate | Distinct suppressed status with a pinned reason in errorMessage (Task 2) |
| T-1XS-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | mitigate | No dependency installs; uses installed web-push, drizzle, zod and native fetch, so no legitimacy audit applies |
