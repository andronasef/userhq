# UserHQ

UserHQ connects customer feedback with a product team's public progress and private planning.

## Language

**Workspace**: A company's shared team and collection of products.
_Avoid_: Company account, board

**Product**: An offering within a workspace with its own customer portal and feedback content.
_Avoid_: Workspace, board

**Feedback board**: The product's collection of customer requests and conversations.
_Avoid_: Workspace inbox

**Request**: A customer suggestion or reported problem on a product's feedback board.
_Avoid_: Ticket (when referring to customer feedback)

**Comment**: A message in a request's conversation.

**Roadmap item**: A planned piece of product work with private planning details and an optional public presentation.
_Avoid_: Request

**Product feature**: One of the portal modules a product offers: Board, Roadmap, Changelog or FAQ.
_Avoid_: Subscription entitlement

**Needs attention**: Shared team work awaiting review or a response to customer feedback.
_Avoid_: Unread

**Handled**: A customer comment resolved for the team by an admin response or an explicit handling action.
_Avoid_: Read

**Personal unread**: A person's indication of activity they have not yet read, independent of the team's handling state.
_Avoid_: Needs attention
