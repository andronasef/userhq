# UserHQ

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

B2B SaaS product teams, agencies, and creators manage feedback; their customers submit and vote on ideas, follow roadmaps, read release notes, and find FAQ answers.

## Product Purpose

Turn customer demand into prioritized work and close the loop publicly: feedback in, roadmap out, changelog shipped.

## Capabilities and Constraints

A branded public portal shares data with private product planning. Preserve OAuth authentication, tenant isolation, private roadmap boundaries, workspace permissions, and customer-configured logos and accents. The web app uses vinext, Tailwind v4, individual Radix primitives, and a decoupled NestJS API. Self-host fonts; no database dependencies in web.

## Brand Commitments

The user supplied UserHQ_Brand_Identity.html and requested autonomous adaptation in its spirit rather than literal duplication. Preserve the single-color human face signature and human, useful, energetic character. Working screens must remain calm and readable.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing implementation and .planning/PROJECT.md establish product truth. Supplied brand document establishes visual authority. No new marketing claims, customer metrics, or testimonials are authorized.
